# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The current version is for its owner, who uses it to manage personal cash flow.

## Product Purpose

Runway brings liquid account balances, scheduled income, and upcoming obligations into a forward cash forecast. It helps its owner understand available cash and anticipate a likely shortfall.

## Positioning

Runway forecasts cash runway from the combination of liquid balances, scheduled income, and bills, rather than only recording past transactions or showing a current balance.

## Operating Context

The product is used by one person for personal finances. It is designed for local or self-hosted operation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Supports PHP-denominated finances.
- The current product is single-owner and non-multitenant.
- Core workflows include account balance tracking, flexible income schedules, bill tracking, balance reconciliation, and transaction capture.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository includes the application, forecasting and transaction code, database schema, and setup documentation. Do not invent testimonials, customer counts, benchmarks, pricing, or external endorsements.

## Product Principles

- Show how current cash, future income, and upcoming obligations affect the forecast.
- Make likely shortfalls visible early enough to support practical decisions.
- Keep workflows focused on the needs of one person managing personal finances.
- Preserve reliable account balances and scheduled obligations as the basis of forecasts.
